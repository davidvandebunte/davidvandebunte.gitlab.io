# Narrow focus

## Estimate value

Generalizes many processes. To make your mind attend to a narrower:
- General world state, see [](./set-deep-goal.md).
- Serialized thoughts, see [](./organize-notes.md).

Build better internal representations in order to make solving future problems more efficient.

By closing windows and tabs on your computer, you free up your limited attention for other tasks.

## Design test

Attend to a smaller set of weights in your mind, typically a set of shared weights.

## Estimate cost

Run `git grep` for all the key words in question, and use or delete your old thoughts. This is the
equivalent of querying your own notes.
