# Set wide goal

That is, should you take the time to add G2 to this graph?

```bash
* c: G1
| * b: G2
|/
* a
```

# Value

Thinking in this direction makes you more of a renaissance man, moving you away from a
specialization. What opportunities are you missing in life? See also the V in [INVEST (mnemonic)](
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INVEST_(mnemonic)).

Said another way, planning widely is about diversification. Do you want to put all your eggs in one
basket and watch it closely, or not? Just like there's more than one way to skin a cat, there's more
than one way to make money (or achieve value, in general).
